  • rgb.info now recognized as an official/educational source by all 5 platforms (up from 0) — the 2025 split is universally acknowledged
  • ChatGPT & Gemini — held or extended June's gains: Gemini names Association members, ChatGPT now leans toward v0.11.1 as the production choice
  • Perplexity — improved further, dropped the rgb.tech security-notice narrative (still vague, not yet clearly correct)
  • Grok — regressed: reversed its June turnaround, back to near-baseline framing on v0.11.1 safety
  • DeepSeek — mixed: held its gains on identity/Association, regressed on the core safety question (language close to March)
What drove the improvement. Two things fed the June–July gains. Indexing infrastructure — Google/Bing sitemaps, IndexNow, meta description and llms.txt/robots.txt fixes across the Kinsta migration — sped up and cleaned the signal AI crawlers (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot) receive from rgb.info. Editorial output and third-party citations — regular blog updates and original articles on rgb.info, plus external mentions and outreach placements on KaleidoSwap, BiHelix, CryptoSmart.it, Bitcoin Magazine, Bitcoin Optech, CKB DevRel, and Bitcoin Tribe — multiplied the recognition signals models use to decide which source is authoritative. Removing disinformation mattered just as much as adding content: Gate.io took down two outdated articles that were actively pulling answers toward rgb.tech.
Why some platforms regressed in July. The Grok/DeepSeek regression concentrates on the safety question (Q2/Q3/Q8) — the one spot where rgb.tech's original security notice is a single, specific, quotable source, while rgb.info's rebuttal is spread across many pages rather than one equally citable page. Both platforms also lean more on live web/social retrieval, making them more sensitive to whatever is freshly indexed — though that's not the full story, since Perplexity is equally live-search-based yet improved over the same period. The likely fix isn't more content volume, but one dedicated page rebutting the 2025 security notice point by point.
